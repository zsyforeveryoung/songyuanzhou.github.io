Hi, I'm Songyuan.

I am a first year ScM candidate in the Department of Neurobiology, Southern University of Science and Technology, College of Life Science, supervised by Zeyu Shen.

I am broadly interested in developing multi-disciplinary methods and theory to address problems that emerge in life science or artificial life system. From my perspective, bioinformatics will be an important development direction for biology research in the 21st century.

Previously, I received an ScB in Statistics and from Department of Statistics and Data Science, Southern University of Science and Technology.

When I'm resting my brain, you probably could find me on steam.
